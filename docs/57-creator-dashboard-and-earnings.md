# Creator dashboard and earnings

The mobile-responsive creator workspace combines notifications, privacy-safe reporting, campaigns, partnership requests, QR access, earnings balances, and payout history. Earnings come from the existing immutable ledger and expose only the creator's 4% share. Customer purchase totals and contact details, merchant escrow, platform revenue, and the customer/platform shares are not returned.

Payouts remain manual. The workspace shows pending, available, held/reserved, scheduled, and paid balances, the next Friday 5:00 PM estimate, processing/failure details, safe external references, and history. A Platform Admin performs the external transfer and records its result.

# v0.33.0 release candidate and private-Pilot approval

## Release notes

Milestone 33 hardens the existing private-Pilot release for one Hetzner Cloud server in Germany behind Cloudflare Free. It adds fail-closed recognition of common placeholder-secret styles, loopback-only Pilot port publishing, repeatable Hetzner/deployment guidance, a bounded synthetic performance harness, and manual CI/container workflow dispatch. It does not add product features or authorize a public launch.

## Existing implementation reused

- Shopper: registration/verification, discovery, Offer checkout creation and approval, history, wallet/cashback, notification and support.
- Creator: onboarding and Platform Admin approval, partnerships, Offer/campaign listing and QR retrieval, earnings and manual payout preparation.
- Business Owner: onboarding/approval, locations/staff, Creator approval, Offers and `OfferReuseRule`, wallet funding, purchases, journals and reporting.
- Supervisor/Cashier: assigned-location authorization, temporary signed QR checkout, duplicate/reuse handling and independent supervisor override.
- Platform Admin: protected approval, suspension, Pilot Operations, support, audit, fraud, dispute/reversal, deposit, payout and reconciliation views.
- Platform: JWT/refresh-token security, active-account and role policies, QR token hashing/tamper rejection, masked phones, validation/rate limiting, safe Problem Details, security headers/CORS/HTTPS, structured audit data, serializable and idempotent financial posting, immutable commission snapshots, balanced wallet/Creator/Shopper/platform journals, notification outbox retries, health/monitoring, maintenance mode, PostgreSQL migrations, backup/restore, Docker/Compose, English/Amharic and accessible Pilot UX.

## Manual verification

Verify each role sees only its own scope; suspended/pending accounts fail closed; expired/tampered QR and changed-payload idempotency retries are rejected; identical retries return the original result; concurrent checkout produces one posting; maintenance returns 503 for cashier checkout while health/admin remain available; API and Worker restart without duplicate financial/outbox work; database loss makes readiness unhealthy and recovery reconnects; notification failures retry then remain observable; SIGTERM produces graceful shutdown logs; external SMS/email/payment and automatic payouts remain disabled.

## Rollback decision criteria

Stop new checkout and roll back the application image for repeatable authorization regression, elevated 5xx/latency, broken role flow, Worker crash loop, or incompatible UI/API behavior. Stop financial traffic immediately for duplicate posting, journal imbalance, incorrect commission/cashback/earnings, data loss, suspected credential exposure or uncontrolled provider calls. Prefer a forward schema fix; do not automatically migrate down. Restore only after incident/change approval and an isolated verification.

## First 24 hours

At launch and 15/30/60 minutes, then hourly: verify live/ready/web/Worker health, 5xx/429 and p95 latency, PostgreSQL capacity/locks, disk/memory/CPU, checkout outcomes and retries, journal reconciliation, wallet/cashback/earnings/platform totals, outbox age/failures, support/disputes, audit events, Cloudflare/Hetzner alerts and backup completion. Record owner, UTC timestamp and safe public/correlation IDs.

## First 7 days

Daily: complete the operational reconciliation and support review; inspect resource trends and slow-query evidence; verify backup age/checksum/off-host copy; review access/audit/security events and Cloudflare traffic; collect role feedback. On days 2 and 7 review capacity, Pilot limits, unresolved incidents, legal/privacy issues and go/no-go status. Do not expand participants or enable public/provider flags without a separate approval.

## Known limitations and unresolved risks

The Pilot is single-server and has an availability/storage failure domain; external providers and automatic payout are off; seed material is operator-generated and environment-local; performance figures require a deployed synthetic environment; Cloudflare Free has limited WAF/rate-limiting controls; restore/server-replacement RPO/RTO must be measured; no public launch, final legal approval, independent penetration test, or security certification is claimed.

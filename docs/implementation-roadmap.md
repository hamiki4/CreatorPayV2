# Implementation Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Foundation

Solution structure, project references, dependency-injection seams, health endpoint, OpenAPI, test projects, frontend shell, local PostgreSQL configuration, and documentation.

## Future milestones

1. Define the domain model, invariants, and persistence boundaries.
2. Implement creator and merchant registration plus Platform Admin approval workflows.
3. Implement merchant creator discovery, promoter requests, and partnership approval.
4. Add permanent creator QR codes and cashier validation of active partnerships.
5. Add customer phone verification, merchant prepaid wallets, and configurable commissions/splits.
6. Add immediate earning notifications, weekly payouts, fraud controls, and repeat-use approval.
7. Add offline cashier workflows and English/Amharic localization.
8. Harden security, observability, deployment, and operational readiness.

Business features above are roadmap items only and are not part of Foundation Milestone 1.

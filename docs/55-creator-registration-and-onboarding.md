# Creator registration and onboarding

Weymela's public entry page offers Sign In plus Customer, Creator, and Merchant registration. Platform Admin registration is deliberately not public.

Creator registration captures legal/public identity, contact and locale, city/zone, biography, categories, a required primary social profile, follower estimate, terms acceptance, and optional non-secret identity and manual-payout metadata. It never requests a bank password, PIN, OTP, or payment credential. Email and phone use the existing development verification provider. The account remains restricted through verification and platform review; an approved creator becomes active.

Social profiles support TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram, YouTube, and Other. A creator must have exactly one primary profile, and a platform/handle combination cannot repeat for that creator. Approved creators can edit public profile and payout-preference metadata; legal identity changes require a review path.
